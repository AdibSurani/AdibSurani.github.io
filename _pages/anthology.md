*An anthology of diversions, inventions & bric-a-brac.*

Call me Adib. Some years ago -- never mind how long precisely -- having little or no money in my purse, I started making a bunch of small random things. Over time, this collection has amassed into quite a lot of stuff: puzzles, CTF challenges, bits of maths, game translations, and various other things that don't fit neatly into any one category. Much of it has ended up scattered across the web, or worse, private forums or dead IRC channels.

This site is my attempt to curate it all in one place.

My full name is Muhammad Adib bin Surani, but [Malay names](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_names) can get very confusing so I often present myself as Adib Surani. I've been married for sixteen years, with three wonderful kids.

I am a mathematician, puzzle hunter, and CTF player. In the puzzle world I'm usually just Adib, but in the hacking scene (be it CTF or game translation), I typically go by the pseudonym Neobeo.

This isn't really meant to be a portfolio as much as it is an anthology: A loose index of things I have made, written, reverse engineered, translated, solved, or otherwise got myself involved in over the years.

## Software & reverse engineering

Most of this work came from wanting to understand old games and formats just enough to bend them into a more convenient shape.

### Open source / reverse engineering

- **[Mobius](https://github.com/AdibSurani/Mobius)** — reverse engineered Mobiclip and related formats; the work later formed the basis of FFmpeg's [MobiClip, FastAudio, MOFLEX and MODS support](https://ffmpeg.org/pipermail/ffmpeg-devel/2021-March/277368.html).
- **[Kuriimu](https://github.com/IcySon55/Kuriimu) / [Kuriimu2](https://github.com/FanTranslatorsInternational/Kuriimu2)** — game-translation and file-format tooling; Kuriimu2 credits Neobeo for development work across compression, encryption, image formats, plugins, performance and related areas.

### Jazz Jackrabbit 2

- **[JJ2+](https://www.jazz2online.com/jcf/showthread.php?t=19489)** — started out as a small app to hook into JJ2 to counteract hacking attempts, and has since turned into a huge mod of the original game thanks to various others.
- **[File-format specifications](https://www.jazz2online.com/jcf/showthread.php?t=15059#post354459)** — documentation of some of the more common file formats in the game, so people can build their own tileset or level editor.
- **[Tileset Extractor](https://www.jazz2online.com/downloads/4087/tileset-extractor/)** — a small C++ app to convert a tileset into a PNG.
- **[Jazz Sprite Dynamite](https://www.jazz2online.com/downloads/4041/jazz-sprite-dynamite/)** — a VB6 app to modify sprites and animations in the game.
- **[Neobeo's Firetruck](https://www.jazz2online.com/downloads/4549/neobeos-firetruck/)** — a level with weird hacks that cannot be done using the standard level editor.
- Later tools **[WebJCS](https://www.jazz2online.com/downloads/6796/webjcs-133/)** and **[MLLE](https://www.jazz2online.com/downloads/7291/multilayer-level-editor-v221a/)** credit the specifications / sound reverse-engineering work.

### Nintendo / game-format tools

- **[DX Degausser](https://gbatemp.net/download/dx-degausser.4613/)** — probably my earliest contribution to the Nintendo DS scene, this tool allows you to swap songs within a Daigasso! Band Brothers DX save file.
- **[Degausser](https://github.com/AdibSurani/Degausser)** — like the above, but for Daigasso! Band Brothers P. Also comes with a version that runs entirely on the 3DS.
- **[Cetera](https://github.com/AdibSurani/Cetera)** — a library to encode and decode 3DS file formats like text, images and archives.
- **[GUI-Investigator](https://github.com/AdibSurani/GUI-Investigator)** — a tool for investigating the complicated GUI format found in The Great Ace Attorney.
- **[RHMPatch](https://github.com/rhmodding/RHMPatch)** — a redirection patch for Rhythm Heaven Megamix to make modding easier.
- **[Backstab](https://github.com/AdibSurani/Backstab)** — Time Travelers storyboard decompiler.
- **[Moonbeam](https://github.com/AdibSurani/Moonbeam)** — MBM to XML converter.
- **[XFSeeker](https://github.com/AdibSurani/XFSeeker)** — XFS file parsing / FSM implementation.
- **[MobiusDebugger](https://github.com/AdibSurani/MobiusDebugger)** — companion/debugging work around Mobius.

### Other software / builds

- **[Equation Solver v3.31](https://web.archive.org/web/20040616032606/http://formulated.net/)** — a school project I wrote in VB6 around 2003-2004, largely modelled after Graphmatica but with a number of more specific features.
- **[Timescraper](https://adib.fourplusfive.com/timescraper/)** — a tool to scrape the Times crossword from a blog and automatically convert it to the `.puz` format, which allows solving collaboratively online.
- **Cocomelon smart button** — Zigbee/MQTT/ADB home automation for launching Maria's favourite videos on Google TV.
